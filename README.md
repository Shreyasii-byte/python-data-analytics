## 🎯 Key Areas of Learning

- **Python Fundamentals:** Data structures, functions, and data manipulation.
- **Data Analysis:** Working with Pandas and NumPy.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Identifying patterns, trends, and relationships.
- **Data Cleaning:** Handling missing values, duplicates, and inconsistent data.
- **Data Visualization:** Creating meaningful charts and visual reports.
- **Business Analytics:** Analyzing sales, revenue, profit, customer behavior, and performance.

## 📈 Featured Project: Retail Sales Analysis

An exploratory analysis of a retail Superstore dataset to understand business performance and uncover actionable insights.

**Key analysis areas:**
- Overall sales, profit, and order performance.
- Category and sub-category analysis.
- Monthly sales trends.
- Regional sales and profitability.
- Customer and product performance.
- Correlation between numerical variables.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook.
